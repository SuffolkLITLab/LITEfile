# Step 6: Add or find case information

**What you will do:** Tell the court where your papers go and what kind of case
it is.

**Before you start:** Upload your documents. If you have an existing case, have
your **case number** ready.

This step is called **Confirm information**. If LITEfile finds a case number,
it selects **File in an existing case** and fills in the number. Choose
**Change** if this is for a new case. Otherwise, answer **Is this for a new or existing court case?**
Choose **Help me decide** if you are not sure.

If LITEfile read your document, some answers are already filled in. Check each
one against your form and fix any mistakes. If you see **We could not read the
details**, type the answers yourself.

Open **Details read from your document** to see other facts LITEfile found.
These are only suggestions. They do not change your answers below. To fix a
mistake inside a PDF, fix your original file and choose **Change files** to replace it.

Each file has its own **Type of document (filing code)**. You can correct the
case details and document types together. Changing the court or case type
may mean you need to choose document options again.

Some cases also ask **Which side of this case are you on?** Pick your side.

## Start a new case

<img src="/img/user-guide/confirm-new-case.png" alt="Confirm information page with New case selected, shared court and case choices, and a separate document type for each PDF." className="doc-screenshot" width="700" loading="lazy" />

1. Choose **New case**.
2. Under **Tell the court what you're filing**, pick:
   - **Court or county:** Answer the questions about the court. Use what's
     printed at the top of your form. (In Vermont: **Court unit or county**.)
   - **Case category:** The big group, like *Small Claims* or *Family*. (In
     Vermont: **Court division**.)
   - **Case type:** The more exact kind of case.
   - **Type of document (filing code) if known:** The name of your document, like *Complaint*.
3. Choose **Continue**.

**Not sure what to pick?** Choose **Help me choose**. LITEfile asks a few easy
questions and fills these in for you.

## File in an existing case

<img src="/img/user-guide/confirm-existing-case.png" alt="Confirm information page showing a detected case number, an Existing case choice with a Change button, and a suggested document type of Appearance." className="doc-screenshot" width="700" loading="lazy" />
<p className="screenshot-caption">Before you confirm the court case, a suggested document type is only a guess.</p>

<img src="/img/user-guide/find-your-case.png" alt="Find your court case page with Circuit Court and Sangamon County chosen and a case number typed in, and a Find my case button." className="doc-screenshot" width="700" loading="lazy" />

1. Choose **Existing case**. If you see a **Suggested type of document (filing code)**, it is our
   best guess from your document. If it looks wrong, check **This doesn't look
   right. I will choose on my own later.** You can pick the document type (filing code) after you confirm your case or in
   [Step 7](./organize-documents.md).
2. Choose **Continue**.
3. On **Find your court case**, pick the court. Answer the court level and
   county questions.
4. Type the **Case number** from your papers. Spaces and dashes are okay.
5. Choose **Find my case**.
6. Check the case name, number and people on **Is this your court case?**
7. If it's right, choose **Yes, this is my case**. If not, choose **No, search
   again**.

<img src="/img/user-guide/is-this-your-case.png" alt="Is this your court case page showing case name Sam Parker vs. Jordan Rivera, the case number, court, category, type and the people already on the case." className="doc-screenshot" width="700" loading="lazy" />

After you confirm your case, the court, category, and case type come from the
court's records. If you go back to **Confirm information**, choose **Change case**
to pick a different case. Under **Your documents**, you can also change each
document's type.

## What you should see

You move to **Check documents**. The step **Confirm case** has a check mark.

## If something is different

- **Your court is not in the list:** LITEfile may not support it yet. See
  [Can I file here?](./can-i-file-here.md)
- **Your case isn't found:** Check the number and the county. Copy the number
  exactly as it is on your papers. If it still fails, call the clerk.
- **You picked the wrong court or case type:** Choose **Edit** or **Change**
  and pick again.
- **The court's case details look wrong:** You can't change the court's records
  here. Call the clerk.
