# Fix a returned filing

**What you will do:** Find out why the clerk sent your papers back. Fix them
and send them again.

**Before you start:** [Sign in](./sign-in.md). Have your original forms handy,
in case you need to change them.

## 1. Read why it was returned

1. Go to **My filings**.
2. Find the filing that says **Returned for changes**.
3. Choose **See details and documents**.
4. Read **Why the court rejected this**.

<img src="/img/user-guide/status-returned.png" alt="Returned for changes filing with a Copy and review filing button and a section called Why the court rejected this." className="doc-screenshot" width="700" loading="lazy" />

If the reason is missing or unclear, call the clerk. Their phone number is
under **Questions about this filing**.

## 2. Fix your papers

Common reasons and fixes:

- **Missing signature or answer:** Fix the form. Save it again as a PDF.
- **Wrong filing code:** Pick a different filing code in LITEfile.
- **Wrong court or case:** Check the court and case number.
- **Missing form:** Add the form the clerk asked for.

## 3. Copy and send again

1. On the filing's page, choose **Copy and review filing**.
2. LITEfile makes a new draft with your answers and documents. It opens on the
   review page.
3. Read **Clerk feedback** and **Before you can submit**.
4. Use **Edit** to change what the clerk asked for. To swap a PDF, upload the
   new one.
5. Choose a payment method or fee waiver again.
6. Check the box for the court's rules. Submit.

<img src="/img/user-guide/correction-review.png" alt="Review your corrected filing page showing clerk feedback and a Before you can submit list of things to fix." className="doc-screenshot" width="700" loading="lazy" />

To pick up later, find the filing in **My filings** and choose **Continue
correction**. After you send it, choose **View corrected submission** to see
the new filing. The old one stays on your list.

## What you should see

A new confirmation number. Your corrected filing goes through clerk review
again.

## If something is different

- **No "Copy and review filing" button:** It only shows when the court has
  returned **every** document in that filing. If some were accepted, don't
  send those again. Call the clerk to ask what to do.
- **There's a deadline:** Ask the clerk if your new filing will count as on
  time. Don't wait.

Learn more about clerk review in [What happens after you file](./after-you-file.md).
