# Step 4: Start a filing

**What you will do:** Open a new filing, or pick up one you started before.

**Before you start:** [Sign in](./sign-in.md). Have your finished forms saved on
your phone or computer.

## The filing home page

After you sign in, you see four choices:

<img src="/img/user-guide/filing-home.png" alt="Filing home page with four choices: Continue a draft, Start a new filing, My filings and Find the documents you need." className="doc-screenshot" width="700" loading="lazy" />

| Choice | Use it to... |
| --- | --- |
| **Continue a draft** | Go back to a filing you started but did not send. Only shows if you have one. |
| **Start a new filing** | Begin sending new papers to the court. |
| **My filings** | See papers you already sent and what the court did. |
| **Find the documents you need** | Find which forms to use. See [Get your forms ready first](./before-you-begin.md). |

## Start a new filing

1. Under **Start a new filing**, choose **Start filing**.
2. LITEfile opens the **Upload** step.

You don't pick "new case" or "existing case" yet. LITEfile asks you after you
upload.

## Or start from a forms guide

If you used **Find documents**, open your guide and choose **Start this
filing** (new case) or **File into my case** (existing case). LITEfile
remembers which kind of case it is.

## What you should see

A row of steps at the top: Start, Upload, Confirm case, Check documents,
Organize documents, People, Fees and Review. The step you are on is
highlighted.

## On a phone

LITEfile works on a phone. The steps stack up so you can scroll.

<img src="/img/user-guide/filing-home-mobile.png" alt="Filing home page on a phone screen." className="doc-screenshot" width="300" loading="lazy" />

## If something is different

- **You want to finish an old filing:** Choose **Continue a draft**. See
  [Save your work and come back](./save-and-return.md).
- **You need to file in a different state:** Open the **Menu** and choose
  **File in a different state**.
