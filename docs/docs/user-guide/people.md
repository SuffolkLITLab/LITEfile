# Step 8: Add or check people

**What you will do:** Tell the court who you are and who else is in the case.

**Before you start:** Have the full names and addresses of the other people in
your case. They are usually on your forms.

## Your information

Check your name, mailing address, email and phone. The court uses this to
contact you. Choose **Continue to parties**.

## New case

<img src="/img/user-guide/choose-role.png" alt="Who is involved in this case page. It suggests the role Plaintiff/Petitioner and lists role choices and a Save role button." className="doc-screenshot" width="700" loading="lazy" />

1. Under **What is your role in this case?**, pick your role. Look for it next
   to your name on your forms. For example, the person who sues is usually the
   **Plaintiff/Petitioner**.
2. Choose **Save role**.
3. Choose **Continue**, or **Add another person**.
4. For each other person, pick their role. Choose **Person** or
   **Organization**. Type their name and address.
5. Choose **Save and continue**.

<img src="/img/user-guide/add-person.png" alt="Add the next party form with role, person or organization, name and mailing address boxes." className="doc-screenshot" width="700" loading="lazy" />

The court often needs someone on each side. If one is missing, LITEfile tells
you.

## Existing case

The court already has a list of people in the case. You can't change those
names here.

1. Under **Who are you filing for?**, check the box next to your name.
2. Check **Email for court notices**.
3. Find your name in the **Party list** and choose **This is me**.
4. Choose **Continue**.

<img src="/img/user-guide/existing-case-parties.png" alt="Existing case people page showing the court's list of people. Jordan Rivera is marked as the defendant the filer is filing for." className="doc-screenshot" width="700" loading="lazy" />

## Filing for someone else

If you are not a party, choose **I am not one of the parties — I am filing for
someone else**. Then pick who you are filing for. Usually, only a lawyer may
file court papers for another adult. Ask a help center before you do this.

## What you should see

Each person shows **Complete**. You then move to **Fees**.

## If something is different

- **A name in the court's list is wrong:** Call the clerk. You can't fix the
  court's record in LITEfile.
- **You don't know someone's address:** Choose **Do I need to list an
  address?** for help.
- **More questions appear:** Some cases need extra details. Answer what you
  can.
