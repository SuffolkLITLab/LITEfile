# Step 3: Sign in and reset your password

**What you will do:** Sign in so you can file.

**Before you start:** You need an activated account. See
[Step 2: Create your account](./create-account.md).

## Sign in

1. Open LITEfile and choose your state.
2. Type your **Email address** and **Password**.
3. Choose **Sign in**.

<img src="/img/user-guide/sign-in.png" alt="Illinois Sign in page with email and password boxes, a Forgot password link, a Sign in button and a Create an account link." className="doc-screenshot" width="600" loading="lazy" />

**What you should see:** The filing home page, with buttons to start a filing
or see your filings.

## Forgot your password?

1. On the **Sign in** page, choose **Forgot password?**
2. Type your email address.
3. Choose **Send reset link**.
4. Open the email. It comes from an address that ends in
   `efilingmail.tylertech.cloud`.
5. Select the link and choose a new password.
6. Go back to LITEfile and sign in.

<img src="/img/user-guide/reset-password.png" alt="Reset your eFile password page with an email box and a Send reset link button." className="doc-screenshot" width="600" loading="lazy" />

## If something is different

- **"That email and password did not match an account":** Check for typos.
  Make sure you picked the right state. Then try resetting your password.
- **"Your account is not activated yet":** Find your activation email and select
  the link. Check your spam folder.
- **No reset email:** Check spam. Wait a few minutes and try again.
- **"We could not reach the court's e-filing system":** The court's system may
  be down. Try again in a few minutes.

## Signing out

Choose the person icon at the top of the page, then choose **Sign out**. Always sign out
on a shared or public computer.
