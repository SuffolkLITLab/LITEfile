# Step 2: Create your account

**What you will do:** Make a free e-filing account for your state's courts.

**Skip this step if** you already have a court e-filing account in your state.
Try signing in with it first. See [Step 3: Sign in](./sign-in.md).

**Before you start:** You need an email address you can check, and your mailing
address.

## Steps

1. Open LITEfile and choose your state.
2. On the **Sign in** page, under **First time?**, choose **Create an account**.
3. Type your full legal name, address, email and phone.
4. Choose a password. Type it again to confirm it.
5. Choose **Create account**.

<p className="screenshot-caption">Use your full legal name and your current address.</p>

## Activate your account

You must activate your account before you can sign in.

1. Open your email.
2. Find the email from the court's e-filing system. It may take a few minutes.
3. Select the activation link in the email.
4. Go back to LITEfile and sign in.

## What you should see

After you choose **Create account**, LITEfile takes you to the **Sign in**
page. A message says your account is registered and asks you to check your
email.

## If something is different

- **No email?** Check your spam or junk folder. Wait a few minutes.
- **"Your account is not activated yet":** Open the activation email and select
  the link first.
- **Your state is missing from the list:** Don't pick a different state. Ask the
  court for help.
- **The email is already in use:** You may already have an account. Try
  [resetting your password](./sign-in.md#forgot-your-password).

## Good to know

Your account is with your state's court e-filing system. A company called
Tyler Technologies runs that system. Each state has its own accounts. If you
file in two states, you need an account in each one.
