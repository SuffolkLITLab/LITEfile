# Step 7: Check and organize your documents

**What you will do:** Make sure all your papers are included. Then tell the
court what each one is.

**Before you start:** Finish [Step 6](./case-information.md).

## Check your documents

This screen lists the files you added.

<img src="/img/user-guide/check-documents.png" alt="Check your documents page with a checked Complaint and a newly added Exhibit, filing rules, a document type choice, and a This copy looks right button." className="doc-screenshot" width="700" loading="lazy" />

1. Open **View PDF** to look at each file. Check every page, signature and
   answer. This is the copy the court gets. If asked, choose **This copy looks
   right**.
2. Forgot something? Choose **Add a missing document**.
3. Choose **Continue to organize**.

Some courts show a list of papers that people **often** file with yours. It is
only a guide. It does not mean your filing is complete or correct.

## Organize your documents

For each file:

1. **Document name:** Give it a clear name, like *Small Claims Complaint*.
2. **Type of document (filing code):** Pick the court's name for this kind of paper. In an
   existing case, you can open **Find my document type** and type a word, like
   "appearance."
3. After choosing a document type, check **Should this document be
   confidential?** and **How this document is filed**, when offered. Follow
   your court’s rules. If there is only one allowed setting, LITEfile shows
   that setting without asking you to choose.
4. Choose **Save and continue**.

<img src="/img/user-guide/organize-existing-case.png" alt="Organize your documents page showing the source of case suggestions, document types, Public access, the court’s Lead Document setting, and three certified copies." className="doc-screenshot" width="700" loading="lazy" />

<img src="/img/user-guide/find-filing-type.png" alt="Find my document type search box with the word appearance typed and a matching document type (filing code)." className="doc-screenshot" width="700" loading="lazy" />
<p className="screenshot-caption">Type a word from your form's title to narrow the list of document types (filing codes).</p>

When you add a missing file on **Check your documents**, check its PDF and
pick its **Type of document (filing code)** right there. You can also open
**Information from the court about filing rules**. Your saved case answers
and AI setting do not change.

If you want extras like a certified copy, open **Certified copy and courtesy
email (optional)**. If you check a service that lets you order more than one,
enter the **Quantity** (it starts at 1). Check the quantity and fee before
you submit.

## Words on this screen

- **Document we read for case suggestions:** The file LITEfile reads to suggest
  case details. If you pick a different file, LITEfile reads it for new suggestions.
  Answers you already saved will not change unless you edit them.
- **Lead Document:** The court's label for an important paper. The court may
  give this label to more than one paper. You usually don't need to change it.
- **Certified copy and courtesy email (optional):** A certified copy is a copy
  the court marks as official. A courtesy email sends a copy to someone. Some
  of these cost money. Most people can skip them.

## If something is different

- **Can't find the right document type (filing code):** Try another word from your form's
  title. Or call the clerk. Don't pick a code just to move on. The wrong code
  can get your filing sent back.
- **The fee changed:** Some document types (filing codes) and services cost more. You will see
  the fee on the next steps.
