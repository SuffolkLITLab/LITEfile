# Save your work and come back

You don't have to finish in one sitting. LITEfile saves your work as you go.

## Three kinds of filings

| Kind | What it is | Where to find it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Draft** | A filing you started but did **not** send. | **Menu** > **My draft e-filings** |
| **Sent filing** | Papers you submitted to the court. | **Menu** > **My filings** |
| **Filing plan** | A list of papers you plan to file. Only in some states. | **Menu** > **My filing plans** |

## When is my work saved?

LITEfile saves when you go to the next step, like when you choose **Continue**
or **Save and continue**. Before you close the page, finish the step you are on.

## Continue a draft

1. Sign in.
2. On the filing home page, open **Continue a draft**. Choose **Choose a
   draft** or **See all my drafts**.
3. Find your filing. Choose **Continue this filing**.

<img src="/img/user-guide/my-drafts.png" alt="My draft e-filings page listing two drafts. Each shows the court, the next step, the number of documents, when it was last worked on, and Continue this filing and Throw this draft away buttons." className="doc-screenshot" width="700" loading="lazy" />

Each draft shows **Next up** so you know where you left off.

## Throw a draft away

Choose **Throw this draft away**. This also deletes the files you uploaded to
it. It does **not** take back anything you already sent to the court.

## If you came from a guided interview

Some guided interviews can send your answers and forms straight to LITEfile.

1. At the end of the interview, choose the option to file with LITEfile.
2. Sign in to LITEfile if asked.
3. LITEfile shows **Your answers and forms are saved. Nothing has been sent to
   the court yet.**
4. If you see **Complete missing details**, choose it and fill in what's
   needed.
5. Open your PDFs and check them.
6. Choose **Continue to final review**. Then finish the
   [review and submit](./review-submit.md) step.

Changing an answer in LITEfile does not change your PDF. If a form needs to
change, choose **Fix a PDF in my interview**. Fix it there and send it to
LITEfile again.

## If something is different

- **Can't find a draft:** Check that you signed in to the right state.
- **The interview link says it expired:** Go back to the interview and send it
  to LITEfile again.
