# Step 9: Pay fees or ask for a fee waiver

**What you will do:** Choose how to handle the court's fee.

**Before you start:** Have a credit card, debit card or bank account. Or, if you
can't afford the fee, have your fee waiver form ready.

## See the fee

At the top, you see **Estimated fees before a waiver**. The exact total shows
after you choose a payment method. You are not charged on this screen.

## Pay with a card or bank account

1. Under **Payment method**, choose **Use a credit or bank account**.
2. Pick a saved card or account. Or choose **Add credit or bank account** and
   follow the steps.
3. Wait for **Confirmed fees** to show the total.
4. Choose **Review your filing**.

<img src="/img/user-guide/fees-pay.png" alt="Choose how to pay court fees page with Use a credit or bank account selected and a saved Visa card chosen." className="doc-screenshot" width="700" loading="lazy" />

There is a small processing fee. It depends on the payment method.

## No fee? You may still need a card

Some filings cost $0. The court may still ask for a card or bank account. If
the confirmed total is $0, you will not be charged.

## Ask for a fee waiver

A fee waiver asks the court to let you file without paying. You may qualify if
you get benefits like SNAP or SSI, or have a low income. Open **Who qualifies
for a fee waiver?** to read your state's rules.

1. Choose **I think I qualify for a fee waiver**.
2. Choose **Add fee waiver document**.
3. Pick the **Filing code** for your waiver form.
4. Under **Who can see this document?**, follow your court's rules. Ask the
   clerk if you are not sure.
5. Upload your filled-out, signed waiver form.
6. Check the PDF. Choose **This copy looks right**.
7. Choose **Review your filing**.

<img src="/img/user-guide/fee-waiver-upload.png" alt="Fee waiver option selected, with a form to add a fee waiver document: filing code, who can see this document, and an upload box." className="doc-screenshot" width="700" loading="lazy" />

**Asking is not the same as getting a waiver.** The judge decides. If the judge
says no, the court may ask you to pay later.

What to include depends on your state. LITEfile tells you on this screen:

- **Illinois:** Include your waiver application, or a waiver order you already
  have. An order usually lasts 12 months.
- **Massachusetts:** Include an Affidavit of Indigency each time you file.
  This is a signed form that says you can't afford court fees.
- **Vermont:** Include your waiver form or order. Ask the court if an
  earlier order covers this filing.

Some cases show **Claim the fee exemption for this case**. Read its note to see
if it fits.

Need the waiver form? See [Get your forms ready first](./before-you-begin.md).

## If something is different

- **Bank account not shown:** Some courts only take cards.
- **Fees could not load:** Wait and try again. Don't submit until you see the
  confirmed fees.
- **You changed a document or filing code:** Check the fees again. They may
  change.
