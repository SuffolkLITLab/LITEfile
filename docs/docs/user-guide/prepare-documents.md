# Step 1: Prepare your documents

**What you will do:** Make sure your finished forms are saved as files that
LITEfile can upload.

**Before you start:** Fill out and sign your forms. See
[Get your forms ready first](./before-you-begin.md).

## Check each file

- [ ] **File type:** PDF (`.pdf`) or Word (`.doc` or `.docx`). LITEfile turns
      Word files into PDFs for the court.
- [ ] **Size:** 10 MB or less for each file.
- [ ] **Signed:** Your signature is on every line that asks for it. A typed
      signature like `/s/ Jordan Rivera` ("/s/" means "signed") is often allowed. Check your form's
      instructions.
- [ ] **Readable:** Pages are right side up and easy to read.
- [ ] **No password:** The file opens without a password.
- [ ] **One form per file:** Keep each form as its own file when you can.

## If your form is on paper

Scan it to a PDF. Many phones can do this:

- **iPhone:** In the **Notes** or **Files** app, choose **Scan Documents**.
- **Android:** In the **Google Drive** app, tap **+**, then **Scan**.

Scan in good light. Make sure every page is there and nothing is cut off.

## If your file is a picture or a Pages file

LITEfile can't take photos (`.jpg`, `.png`) or Apple Pages files. Save or
"print" them as a PDF first. On most computers, choose **Print**, then
**Save as PDF**.

## Protect private information

Courts often require you to hide private numbers. In Illinois, you must remove
all but the last 4 digits of Social Security numbers and bank or card numbers.
LITEfile shows you these rules again before you submit. If the court needs the
full number, there may be a special form for it. Ask the clerk or a help
center.

## What you should have

A folder on your phone or computer with each finished form saved as a PDF or
Word file.

## If something is different

- **File is too big:** Scan in black and white, or at a lower quality.
- **Form has missing answers after you save it:** Use **Print**, then
  **Save as PDF**, and upload that copy instead.
- **You are not sure the form is right:** See [Get help](./get-help.md).
