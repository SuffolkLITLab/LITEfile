# File step by step

Pick the checklist that fits you. Each step links to a short guide with
pictures. You can print this page and check off each step.

Not sure which one you need? See [New case or existing case?](./can-i-file-here.md#new-case-or-existing-case)

## Start a new case

Use this if there is **no case number** yet.

1. [ ] [Prepare your documents](./prepare-documents.md)
2. [ ] [Create your account](./create-account.md), if you don't have one
3. [ ] [Sign in](./sign-in.md)
4. [ ] [Start a filing](./start-filing.md)
5. [ ] [Upload your documents](./upload-documents.md)
6. [ ] [Pick the court and type of case](./case-information.md#start-a-new-case)
7. [ ] [Check and organize your documents](./organize-documents.md)
8. [ ] [Add the people in the case](./people.md#new-case)
9. [ ] [Pay fees or ask for a fee waiver](./fees.md)
10. [ ] [Review and submit](./review-submit.md)
11. [ ] [Check your filing status](./check-status.md)
12. [ ] [Serve the other side](./serve-papers.md#starting-a-new-case), as the court tells you

## File in an existing case

Use this if your papers have a **case number**.

1. [ ] [Prepare your documents](./prepare-documents.md)
2. [ ] [Create your account](./create-account.md), if you don't have one
3. [ ] [Sign in](./sign-in.md)
4. [ ] [Start a filing](./start-filing.md)
5. [ ] [Upload your documents](./upload-documents.md)
6. [ ] [Find your court case](./case-information.md#file-in-an-existing-case)
7. [ ] [Check and organize your documents](./organize-documents.md)
8. [ ] [Pick who you are filing for](./people.md#existing-case)
9. [ ] [Pay fees or ask for a fee waiver](./fees.md)
10. [ ] [Plan how to send copies to the other side](./serve-papers.md#filing-in-a-case-that-already-started)
    and fill out the proof of service on your form
11. [ ] [Review and submit](./review-submit.md)
12. [ ] [Check your filing status](./check-status.md)

## After you submit

Submitting is not the last step. A clerk still has to accept your papers. See
[What happens after you file](./after-you-file.md).

:::note About the pictures
The pictures in this guide come from LITEfile in Illinois, using a made-up
person and case. Screens in Massachusetts and Vermont look almost the same.
Some choices and fees are different in each court.
:::
