# Get help

## You are not alone

Most people in court cases like evictions, small claims and family cases do
**not** have a lawyer. In many of these courts, about 9 out of 10 people
represent themselves. Courts and legal aid groups have help made for you.

## Who to ask

| If you need help with... | Ask... |
| --- | --- |
| Which forms to use, or what to write in them | A court help center or legal aid group |
| Whether you should file, or what will happen in your case | A lawyer or legal aid group |
| Why the clerk sent your papers back | The clerk's office for your court |
| Hearings, deadlines and court rules | The clerk's office or a court help center |
| LITEfile not working | LITEfile support (see below) |

Court staff and help centers **can** explain the rules and forms. They **can't**
tell you what to do in your case. Only a lawyer can give you legal advice.

## Help in your state

### Illinois

- **Illinois Court Help:** call **833-411-1121**, or visit
  [ilcourthelp.gov](https://www.ilcourthelp.gov/).
- **Legal aid:** [Illinois Legal Aid Online — Get legal help](https://www.illinoislegalaid.org/get-legal-help).
- **Court self-help:** [Illinois Courts self-help](https://www.illinoiscourts.gov/self-help/).

### Massachusetts

- **Court Service Centers:** free help with forms and court steps.
  [Find a Court Service Center](https://www.mass.gov/orgs/court-service-centers).
- **Legal aid and information:** [MassLegalHelp](https://www.masslegalhelp.org/).
- **Forms with guided interviews:** [CourtFormsOnline](https://courtformsonline.org/).

### Vermont

- **Legal aid and information:** [VTLawHelp](https://vtlawhelp.org/) and
  [Legal Services Vermont](https://legalservicesvt.org/).
- **Court self-help:** [Vermont Judiciary self-help](https://www.vtcourts.gov/self-help).

### Anywhere in the U.S.

[Legal Services Corporation](https://www.lsc.gov/about-lsc/what-legal-aid/i-need-legal-help)
can help you find a legal aid office near you.

## Tips if you are filing on your own

- **Write down deadlines.** Look for dates on any papers you got.
- **Keep copies** of everything you file and everything you get.
- **Save your confirmation number** after you submit.
- **Check your email**, including spam, for messages about your filing.
- **Ask early.** Help centers can get busy right before deadlines.
- **Be on time** for any hearing. Bring your papers.

## LITEfile help

Inside LITEfile, look at the bottom of any page for **Get more help**, and a
**Call** link if your state has one. Choose **Read the guide for this page** to
open the matching page of this guide. Filing status pages also show the clerk's phone
number.

For technical problems with LITEfile, email the
[Suffolk LIT Lab](mailto:litlab@suffolk.edu). Tell us what page you were on,
what you clicked and any error message. Don't send court papers or private
information by email.
