# Words to know

**Accepted** — The clerk took your papers. They are now part of the court
record. This does not mean the judge agreed with you.

**Case number** — The number the court gives each case. It is usually at the
top of court papers. Example: `2026SC001234`.

**Case type** and **case category** — How the court sorts cases. The category is
the big group, like *Small Claims*. The type is more exact.

**Clerk** — A court worker who checks and keeps court papers.

**Confidential** — Hidden from the public. Only mark a paper confidential if a
court rule or court order says so.

**Confirmation number** or **envelope number** — The number for one
submission. You get it after you submit. Use it when you call the clerk.

**Draft** — A filing you started in LITEfile but did not send.

**E-filing** — Sending papers to the court over the internet.

**Existing case** — A case that already has a case number.

**Fee waiver** — A request to file without paying the court fee. The judge
decides if you get it.

**File-stamped copy** — The court's copy of your paper, marked with the date
the court received it. Save it.

**Filing code** — The court's name for a kind of paper, like *Complaint* or
*Appearance*.

**Hearing** — A meeting with a judge about your case. It may be in person or
online.

**Lead Document** — The court's label for an important paper in a filing.

**Main document** — The paper your filing is mostly about.

**New case** — A case that does not have a case number yet.

**Order** — A judge's written decision.

**Party** — A person or group in the case, like the plaintiff or defendant.

**Plaintiff/Petitioner** — The person who starts the case.

**Defendant/Respondent** — The person the case is against.

**Returned** (or **rejected**) — The clerk sent your papers back to fix.

**Service** — Giving the other side a copy of your papers. See
[Send copies to the other side](./serve-papers.md).

**Tyler Technologies** — The company that runs the court's e-filing system.
Some emails about your filing come from it.
