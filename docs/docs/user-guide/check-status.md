# Step 11: Check your filing status

**What you will do:** See what the court did with your papers.

**Before you start:** [Sign in](./sign-in.md) to the same state you filed in.

## Steps

1. On the filing home page, choose **View filings**. Or open **Menu**, then
   **My filings**. This opens **My cases**.
2. Find your case. Choose **See details and documents**.
3. Read the status at the top.

## What the status means

| Status | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **In progress** | Your filing is being sent. | Wait. |
| **Received by the e-filing system** | The system got it. The clerk has not looked yet. | Wait. |
| **Under review** | A clerk is checking it. | Wait. |
| **Reviewed** | The clerk is done. The court hasn't posted a decision. | Wait. |
| **Accepted** | The court took your papers. | Download your court copy. Do the next steps. |
| **Returned for changes** | The clerk sent it back. | Read why. See [Fix a returned filing](./fix-returned-filing.md). |
| **Did not go through** | It didn't reach the court. | Contact support before you try again. |
| **Stopped** | The filing was canceled. | Read the details. Call the clerk if you didn't expect this. |

The page may say how long review usually takes. That is a guess, not a
promise.

If you sent more than one document, each one can get its own answer. One may be
accepted while another is returned.

## Save your court copies

When a filing is **Accepted**, look under **Documents in this filing** for
**The court's file-stamped copy**. Download it. It shows the court got your
papers and the date.

<img src="/img/user-guide/status-accepted.png" alt="Accepted filing with links to the copy you sent and the court's file-stamped copy." className="doc-screenshot" width="700" loading="lazy" />

The court keeps these copies online for **90 days**. Save them on your phone
or computer.

## Emails about your filing

You may get emails when your filing is sent, accepted or returned. Some come
from the court's e-filing system (Tyler Technologies). You might get more than
one email about the same filing. That does not mean you filed or paid twice.

Emails can be late or go to spam. **Always check My cases** to be sure.

## If something is different

- **Your filing isn't listed:** Make sure you signed in to the right state.
- **No change for a long time:** Call the clerk. Have your confirmation
  (envelope) number ready.
- **Status says Accepted, but you still have questions:** Accepted means the
  clerk took the papers. It does not mean the judge agreed with you.
