# Step 5: Upload your documents

**What you will do:** Add your finished forms to the filing.

**Before you start:** Have your forms saved as PDF or Word files. See
[Step 1: Prepare your documents](./prepare-documents.md).

## Steps

1. Choose **Choose PDF or Word files or drag them here**.
2. Pick your files. You can pick more than one.
3. Choose **Upload selected files**.
4. Wait while LITEfile reads your first file.
5. When each file says **Ready**, choose **Confirm information**.

<p className="screenshot-caption">Each file shows Ready when it is uploaded. Choose View PDF to see it.</p>

## Which file goes first?

Upload your **main document** first. This is the form that asks the court to
do something, like a complaint, petition, motion or appearance. LITEfile reads
the first file to fill in some answers for you.

You can add more papers later, such as a fee waiver form or exhibits.

## Reading your documents with AI

LITEfile can use AI to read your first file and suggest answers, like the court
and case type. You always check the answers next.

If you don't want this, check **Do not use AI to read my documents**. Then you
type the answers yourself. Choose **Learn more about how we use AI** for
details.

## Court rules

Open **Information from the court about filing rules** to read the court's
notes. You will see the court's rules again before you submit.

## What you should see

Your files listed under **Your documents**, each marked **Ready**.

## If something is different

- **Wrong file:** Choose **Remove** next to it. Then upload the right one.
- **Need to swap a file:** Open **Replace a document**, pick the old file, then
  upload the new one.
- **File won't upload:** Check it is a PDF or Word file under 10 MB with no
  password.
- **"Print your file to PDF and upload that copy":** Open the file, choose
  **Print**, then **Save as PDF**. Upload the new copy.
